V04 IDENTITY HARDENING STATUS (2026-09-07)
==================================================

IMPLEMENTED

* IdentityState is independent of the tracking state: UNKNOWN, CANDIDATE,
  CONFIRMED, and RETAINED.
* Face evidence is the one-frame confirmation path. Body-only evidence must be
  high, unambiguous, and consistent for multiple frames before confirmation.
* Canonical identity ownership prevents a second track from claiming an enrolled
  identity on body-only evidence; UNKNOWN is preferred when evidence conflicts.
* Confirmed identity is retained across short face loss/recovery, expires after
  a configurable retention window, and is released when the owner is deleted.
* Enrollment gallery remains immutable; only bounded runtime track galleries are
  updated by tracking logic.

VERIFIED

* 20 unit tests pass, including strong face confirmation, body-only candidate
  rejection, repeated body evidence, canonical-owner protection, and retention.
* Compilation and identity-related Settings environment override pass.

  so its existing valid-embedding and similarity/margin gates are used. Raw
